A duel of light and moths — an original card game for the make.fun bounty
best-card-game-20261122-1826 ("Make the best card game").

You are the Lantern Keeper. Sable, the Moth Queen, wants your light. Each of
you guards 30 lanterns. Every round you each play 3 cards, one at a time,
into three rows — Ember, Tide and Gale. A card played into the row of its own
suit is worth +4. When the round ends, each row is compared and the winner
deals damage equal to the difference. Lantern cards heal you; Tempest cards
blow away the strongest enemy card on the table. First to 0 lanterns loses.

Play it: serve `game/` with any static server and play with mouse or keyboard
(1–5 pick a card, Q/W/E pick a row, Enter plays, Esc cancels). No network, no
build step.

Files: `game/index.html` + `game/style.css` + `game/src/` (deck, rules, AI,
rendering, sound, wiring). Screenshots are real renders of the game.
